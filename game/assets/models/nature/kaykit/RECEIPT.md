# KayKit Forest Nature

Source: https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest. Kay Lousberg, CC0-1.0; downloaded staging pack, admitted 2026-10-08. Selected models only, original geometry and shared 1024px atlas. Original glTF/bin/PNG repacked losslessly into self-contained GLB. No density, seed, spacing or gameplay changes. Source and output hashes plus the sixteen-variant map are in source-receipt.json. Sizes and bottom-centre anchors are normalized at runtime through the world-scale standard.
