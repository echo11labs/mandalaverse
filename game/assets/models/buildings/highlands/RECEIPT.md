# Highlands selected building pieces

VisionsMind, https://visionsmind.itch.io/highlands-fantasy-pack-3d, CC0-1.0 per publisher. Admitted 2026-10-08 from the downloaded staging library. Selected Hut, WoodCrate, Barrel, WoodFence and Axe only. Blender 5.2 background CLI imported FBX, removed all UCX_ collision objects, joined/baked child transforms and exported GLB. Pillow converted source maps to RGB 1024px PNG with Lanczos resizing; base colour, roughness and normal embedded. Original alpha/metal maps were omitted for opaque timber/stone pieces. Source/output hashes and stripped objects are in conversion.json. Existing simple Godot picking shapes remain authoritative.

European-fantasy placeholders and kitbash parts, **not final Newari architecture or historical reconstruction**. No added roles, inventory or simulation fencing.
