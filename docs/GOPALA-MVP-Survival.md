# MVP survival slice

The player-facing MVP scene is `game/scenes/mvp.tscn`; it inherits the Kathmandu landscape and enables the survival systems. The landscape scene remains an isolated engineering fixture. The main project entry now opens the MVP, paused, with eight settlers and initial timber/stone/thatch plus 48 food and 64 water. The prototype balance is data-driven in `game/data/mvp_policy.json`. Values are tuning assumptions, not historical claims.

Use the visible tempo controls to start/pause, select workers and assign supply roles, then place buildings from the build bar and right-click a site with a free builder. Dwellings admit four settlers each after completion. The inspection panel shows health, needs and housing; the left panel shows food/water minutes at the configured population consumption rate (an estimate excluding travel delays). A new-settlement button starts a fresh paused run. Save first to retain the current one.

Every 120 logical ticks a settler requires a meal and a drink. They route to a reachable store holding food or water and consume one actual unit at arrival. Competing diners re-check quantity; reserved construction stock cannot be eaten. Food prefers wild food and then milk. Consumption journals record resource sinks. After a 180-tick overdue grace, health declines by two every 30 ticks; health recovers on supplied ticks and low health slows walking. Completed homes are assigned deterministically without exceeding capacity. Households/family simulation belongs to a later milestone.

Needs release pre-pickup gathering reservations. Existing carried goods finish delivery. Builders retain site reservations, return an existing load to real storage, take their break and resume the unfinished site afterward. Collectors waiting for a missing good may gather that good as a fallback, so the final food/water carrier can recover supplies. Unreachable stock is reported separately from empty stock. Need journeys cannot be redirected into remote consumption by manual movement.

A completed water point creates a source at a free adjacent grid cell. It exposes up to twelve units and replenishes missing units every 60 logical ticks. Source generation is journaled and cannot be accelerated by rendering or repeated setup. Removing its producer stops new supply while preserving any existing carried/pledged water. Water still needs physical collection and delivery. This abstracts a reliable local well; surveyed groundwater and water quality are outside this prototype.

Need state, home links, health, water source counters and interrupted builder targets survive schema 2 saves. The separate MVP slot is `user://saves/mvp.json`; policy/content/scale compatibility is fingerprinted. Saves from before changed definitions and standards are rejected. The full gate includes `run_survival_tests.gd`: physical consumption, needs save/load, four-person home capacity/removal, water source generation, fallback hauling, health decline, carried builder interruption/resumption and malformed health rejection. It also checks the actual eight-settler scene. These tests are engineering evidence, not the 15-minute exported MVP acceptance.

Block 5H remains active: cattle/pasture/tending/milk, complete scenario objectives and packaged acceptance still remain. Final art, localization and release performance/resolution checks also remain; no complete MVP claim is made by this slice.
