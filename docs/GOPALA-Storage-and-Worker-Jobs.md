# Stockpile inventories and persistent collection jobs

Select a villager and use **Timber**, **Stone**, **Thatch**, **Wild food**, **Fodder**, **Water**, **Milk**, **Herd** or **Stop job** in the inspection panel. Assigned collectors repeatedly choose available sources and switch after depletion. They reserve one source unit and one space unit in a specific reachable store before walking. Stores are ordered by distance with stable-ID tie breaking; route checks exclude inaccessible destinations. Several workers cannot promise the same final slot. Full stores pause collection; jobs resume when consumption opens room. Pause freezes dispatch and physical work. Stop job/Escape finish an existing carried delivery before stopping; before pickup they release the reservation.

The Kathmandu settlement now enables individual store inventories. The camp initially owns the seeded settlement stock. The HUD aggregate ledger projects free goods across stores, while selecting a store shows its own stock, capacity and incoming promises. Construction allocations remain at their recorded origin until pickup and continue occupying its capacity. Completed stockpiles add empty inventory and immutable definition limits (currently 200 per admitted good); blueprints have no storage capacity. The scenario camp limits are timber 500, stone 1,000, thatch 100, water 200 and wild-food/fodder/milk 100 each. These are prototype balance values.

Builders reserve all required materials from reachable stores holding them, then physically collect each load from its origin. Labour starts after all requirements arrive. Cancelling returns unpicked reservations to their original store; carried goods return to a reachable store with room. Delivered goods remain at the cancelled site as recoverable sources. Removing a store spills its free and unpicked reserved goods at its position, removes them from available totals and releases affected blueprint promises. Builders wait for replacement supplies and resume after restocking. Collectors can recover dropped timber, stone, thatch, wild food and fodder. Water carriers collect from completed water-point sources in the MVP. Milk carriers collect physically produced milk at cattle. Herders fetch water/fodder from real stores and deliver it to cattle before production; see the cattle/scenario guide.

A capacity reduction after pickup retains carried goods in a waiting state. Removing a haul destination redirects toward another reachable completed store. With no usable destination, the load/task waits without remote credit. Schema 2 saves store partitions, construction origins, roles, reservations and recovery states; routes are rebuilt. Aggregate totals must equal free stock partitions, and construction origin totals must equal blueprint reservations. Older schemas are rejected. Explicit authorized/debug stock transactions update both partitions and totals; these APIs can supply over-capacity goods, which stop new deposits until space opens.

Small legacy engineering fixtures retain pooled storage unless they explicitly enable locations. The live Kathmandu scene and the located inventory suite use individual inventories. Store-to-store hauling, per-store filter controls, general job priorities, general priority dispatch and transport controls remain outstanding.

`run_stockpile_inventory_tests.gd` checks competing destination reservations, local delivery, conservation on physical ticks, actual builder origins, carrying save/load, cancellation recovery, removal of reserved stock, replacement supply, multi-material dwelling hauling before labour and aggregate mismatch rejection. `run_worker_job_tests.gd` also covers persistent roles, paused dispatch, depletion switching, capacity-driven restart, safe stopping and actual HUD controls. Both run in the full verification gate. The separate MVP scenario test covers the exported fifteen-minute objective; these inventory checks do not replace manual approval of final art.

The forage slice seeds eight patches per new category using resource-specific deterministic offsets, with water/slope/access and cross-resource spacing checks. Select a worker and right-click a patch for repeated manual collection, or choose its persistent role. Patches have finite stock: no seasonal regrowth or renewable yield is implemented. `run_forage_tests.gd` checks pause, physical conservation, carried saves, full-store restart, repeated manual orders, live plant views, world picking, role controls and idempotent seeding.
