# GOPALA asset pipeline

Raster prototypes are admitted through `game/assets/manifest.json`. Record stable IDs, relative source paths, dimensions, SHA-256, origin and review status. The content audit loads raw PNG data and checks those values. Current images are original procedural prototypes, not final production art. Concept PNG references at the project root are not automatically admitted gameplay assets.

The scenic landscape uses native procedural meshes and authored shaders instead of imported raster art. Its source receipt is the model and scene scripts in `game/core/landscape/` and `game/scenes/landscape/`, plus `game/assets/materials/`. Store screenshots under `reports/`; screenshots are review evidence, not new gameplay content.

Before replacing prototypes, preserve source files, prompts where applicable, commercial-use rights, attribution requirements and review decisions. Check palette, ground anchors, logical footprint, visibility at the gameplay camera, construction stages and neighbor overlap. A good individual image does not establish correct game integration. Do not label unreviewed content as historical evidence or final Nepali artwork.

Keep generated decorative terrain/trees separate from authoritative source inventories. Future gathering requires stable source IDs, available quantities, reservations and save state, regardless of the visual mesh or sprite used.
